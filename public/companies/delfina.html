Senan Ebrahim '08
Delfina
Delfina is a pregnancy care platform co-founded by physician-scientist Senan Ebrahim, who holds an MD and PhD in Computational Neuroscience from Harvard University. The platform uses proprietary predictive models to help care teams assess patient risk profiles and personalize care plans to improve maternal and child health outcomes. Ebrahim previously founded Hikma Health, a nonprofit providing a free health data platform for refugee and low-income populations.
