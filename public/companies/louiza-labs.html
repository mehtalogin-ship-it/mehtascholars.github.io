Louiza Labs
Larissa Tyagi is the Founder and CEO of Louiza Labs (YC S25), whose physics-driven AI engine simulates human biology to train and test medical devices and robots without real patients or hard-to-access clinical data. Before Louiza, Tyagi was a software engineer at Apple. She holds dual degrees in computer information systems and business from Carnegie Mellon University.
