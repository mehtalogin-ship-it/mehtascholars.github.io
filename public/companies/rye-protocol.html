Arjun Bhargava '10
Rye Protocol
Arjun is a Co-Founder of Rye, an e-commerce API platform that enables merchants to plug in their inventory and sellers to access it through a unified, decentralized network. The platform integrates one-click checkout, Amazon and Shopify product data, and affiliate program connections. Prior to founding Rye, Arjun held technical roles at Goldman Sachs and Reddit. The company raised $14 million in 2022, led by a16z Crypto.
