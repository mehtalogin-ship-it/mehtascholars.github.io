Our Venture Investment Committee are our final decision makers who review alumni founded companies for investment.

Venture Advisory Committee

Our Venture Advisory Committee are professional venture capitalists who utilize their experience in various industries in order to support our Mehta Scholars in conducting due diligence on companies and reviewing their reports for official submission.

Our Entrepreneurship Advisory Committee are an expert group of founders and venture capitalists who support our founders through developing and solidifying their companies.
