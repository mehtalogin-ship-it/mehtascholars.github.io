What we do

Student analysts for The Harker Venture Pool

Top Business & Entrepreneurship students are selected as Mehta Scholars for their 12th-grade year.

01 · Research

Find and study alumni founders

Scholars identify Harker alumni founders, meet with them, and write preliminary and in-depth reports on their companies.

02 · Invest

Decide with the committees

Reports are refined with the Venture Advisory Committee, and the Venture Investment Committee decides whether to invest from The Harker Venture Pool.

03 · Support

Connect founders to help

Scholars promote alumni founders and connect them with VCs, angel investors, entrepreneurs, and business and technology professionals.
